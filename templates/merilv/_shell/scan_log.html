{% load merilv_ui %}{% comment %} The scan log, inside the scan frame — every attempt this session, accepted or rejected, filterable and searchable, directly under the box it was scanned into. It used to be a modal behind a "Check Logs" button, with a separate cut-down "runtime" log on the console. So the two questions a picker asks after every scan — did that land, and what has been refused so far — were answered in two different places, one of them a click away, and the always-visible copy was the one that could not show a rejection's detail. One log, always open, is the answer to both. Nothing about the filtering changed: the same data-status/data-code/ data-delivery contract and the same wireScanFilter() in static/merilv/js/datatable.js, which is document-scoped rather than root-scoped. scanner.js prepends live rows here (scanLogTargets()). The badge counts describe the WHOLE session, not the rendered slice: only `feed_limit` rows are written into the DOM, and data-mv-scanfilter-over-* carries what the cap left out so the filter's own tally can add it back. Without that a 769-scan session reported "All 200 / Scanned 200 / Rejected 0". {% endcomment %}

The count is what makes a growing pile of rejections noticeable while it is still worth stopping for. {% endcomment %}
