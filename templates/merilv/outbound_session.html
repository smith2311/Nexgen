{% comment %} ── Scan error popup ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── One node, reused for every error, never cloned. That is the whole answer to "multiple errors could trigger multiple popups, potential crash": a paste of 2,000 codes with 400 rejections cannot open 400 of anything, because there is only ever this one element and the last error rewrites it. Deliberately NOT a .mv-modal-scrim. That class is what refocusScanInput() and the console's keydown handler check before giving the keyboard back, so a real modal here would take the scan input away from a picker holding a gun — they would have to put it down and click to carry on. This floats above the page, takes no focus, and the next scan replaces it. {% endcomment %}
{% comment %} The serial the error is about, given a line of its own above the barcode rather than being left inside the message text. A picker reads this with a carton in their hand.
