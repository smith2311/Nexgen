The six structural masters each render as a flat table on their own tab, which is why the hierarchy was invisible: a plant row showed its org code but nothing showed the shape of the whole thing. This is that shape. Each level carries its own colour (violet company / blue sales org / green plant / amber storage) and its own indent guide, so depth is readable at a glance rather than counted. A plant mapped to more than one sales org appears under each of them — that is the point of PlantOrgMapping, and a storage location under such a plant legitimately appears more than once too.
