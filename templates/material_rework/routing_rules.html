Routing Rules

Decide who reviews a rework batch, and in what order. A flow runs left to right and ends where the material actually moves — Production (which books the slot) or Warehouse (straight into the rework round trip, no review).

A Type of Rework rule beats a Requested By rule. Assigning a type such as Damage is how you send a batch straight to whoever should handle it, whoever raised it. Editing a rule affects new uploads only — every batch keeps the flow it started on, so reordering a flow can never re-route work already in progress.
