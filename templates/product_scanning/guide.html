What is Stock Audit?
Platform purpose and your role

Stock Audit is Meril's internal platform for tracking medical device consignment stock at hospital locations. Salespersons scan products on-site; the warehouse team reviews, confirms, and archives each session. The system also tracks expiry dates, records hospital-to-hospital transfers, and provides a full audit trail of every action taken.

Step 2 — Camera Mode
Using your device's camera to scan barcodes

Camera Mode is the default input method. The app reads GS1 barcodes and automatically extracts serial number, GTIN, expiry date, batch/lot number, and material code from each scan.

1

Select the Camera tab

At the top of the scanner area, make sure Camera is selected. Grant camera permission if the browser asks — this is required for the scanner to work.

2

Point the camera at a barcode

Hold your device 10–15 cm from the barcode. Keep it steady and ensure the label is well-lit. The scanner auto-captures and beeps/vibrates when it reads successfully — you do not need to press anything.

3

Product is added to the list

Parsed fields (serial number, GTIN, product name, batch, expiry) appear and the item is added to the scanned list below. The camera immediately resumes scanning the next product.

4

Use manual entry if the camera can't read the barcode

Click "Manual Entry" below the scanner. A panel opens where you can either paste the raw barcode string or fill individual fields — Serial Number, GTIN, Material Code, Product Name, Batch, Expiry Date, Received Date, and Quantity.

5

Review and delete errors

Scans that fail to auto-submit (duplicate serial, parse error) appear in the Error Tray below the scanner. You can retry or dismiss each failed scan from there. You can also delete any successfully scanned product from the scanned list using the trash icon, as long as the session is still active.

✅ Better scan tips: Increase screen brightness, clean the camera lens, avoid reflective or curved surfaces, and try holding the device at a slight angle if the barcode is highly reflective or printed on a foil label.
⚠️ Do not close the browser tab or navigate away mid-session. Products already saved are safe on the server, but the in-progress scan queue held in memory will be lost if you refresh.
Step 2 — Scanner Gun Mode
Using a Bluetooth or USB barcode scanner gun

If you have a dedicated Bluetooth or USB barcode scanner gun, use Scanner Gun Mode for faster and more accurate scanning — especially in low-light environments where the phone camera struggles.

1

Switch to the Scanner Gun tab

Click the Scanner Gun tab at the top of the scanner area. A dark-themed input field appears. This field is auto-focused so it captures gun input the moment you pull the trigger.

2

Pair your scanner gun with the device

Connect the scanner gun via Bluetooth or USB before scanning. When you pull the trigger, the gun sends barcode data as keyboard input into the focused field.

3

Scan each product

Pull the trigger on each product barcode. Scans are queued and processed automatically. A preview of queued items appears above the input field. Products are submitted to the server one by one as the queue processes.

4

Use the manual field grid if a field is missing

In Gun Mode you can also switch to a manual field grid — individual input boxes for Serial Number, GTIN, Material Code, Product Name, Batch, Expiry, Received Date, and Quantity. Useful when a barcode is partially unreadable or a single field needs correction before submitting.

✅ Keep the dark input field focused at all times. If you tap elsewhere on the screen the field loses focus and gun input will not register. Tap the dark field again to re-focus before resuming scanning.
2-Scan Workflow
When the serial number is on a separate barcode from the product details

Some Meril products carry two barcodes: one containing only the serial number, and a second containing product details (GTIN, batch, expiry date). The app handles this automatically — you just scan both barcodes and it merges them.

Serial-first scenario
  • Scan the serial number barcode first.
  • Banner: "Serial Number Captured — now scan the product details barcode."
  • Scan the product details barcode.
  • Both scans merge and the product is saved.
Details-first scenario
  • Scan the product details barcode first.
  • Banner: "Now scan the Serial Number barcode."
  • Scan the serial number barcode.
  • Merges automatically and saves.
Manual Override Modal

If you cannot scan the second barcode a modal appears with three options:

OptionWhat it does
Type Serial NumberA text field lets you manually enter the serial number from the product label.
No Serial NumberConfirms this product has no serial number. An internal placeholder is generated (same as the No-Serial toggle — see below).
CancelDiscards the partial scan so you can re-scan the product from scratch.
ℹ️ The 2-scan workflow works in both Camera and Scanner Gun mode. The app remembers the first barcode while you scan the second — no special steps are required.
No-Serial Items (Consumables / Lot-Based Products)
Scanning products like balloons and catheters that have no individual serial number

Consumables produced by batch/lot — such as balloons and catheters — have a GTIN and lot number but no individual serial number per unit. Use the No-Serial toggle when scanning these products to prevent the serial number modal from interrupting your workflow.

1

Enable the No-Serial toggle

Look for the yellow "Products Without Serial Numbers" toggle near the top of the scanner area. Tap it to turn it ON — a yellow indicator confirms it is active.

2

Scan the product barcode normally

The system detects there is no serial number and generates an internal placeholder. The product appears in the scanned list with its GTIN, batch, and expiry — the placeholder is never visible to you.

3

Duplicate detection uses GTIN + Batch + Expiry

For no-serial items the app detects duplicates by matching GTIN, batch number, and expiry date together. Scanning the same lot barcode twice will be rejected as a duplicate.

4

Turn the toggle OFF before scanning serialised products again

Tap the toggle again to disable No-Serial mode. Leaving it ON while scanning serialised products may generate unexpected placeholders.

⚠️ If the serial number modal appears unexpectedly for a no-serial product, you can also choose "No Serial Number" directly from that modal — the effect is the same as using the toggle.

Session Cards

Each session appears as a card showing: hospital name, session/batch ID, salesperson name, product count, date, and a status badge. A remark flag on the card indicates warehouse remarks have been added. Click any card to open the Session Detail view. Quick action buttons on Awaiting cards: Force Complete and Reject.

Session Status Lifecycle
All statuses, meanings, and how sessions transition between them
Normal Flow
Active → Awaiting Confirmation → Closed
Exception Flows
Awaiting ↔ On Hold | Awaiting → Active (Reject) | Active → Cancelled (Terminate)
Active Salesperson is currently scanning at the hospital. No warehouse action needed yet. Use Force Complete if the salesperson has stopped but not submitted.
Awaiting Salesperson submitted. Action required: review the product list, upload a confirmation document, and close the session. Use Reject to send it back if data is wrong.
Closed Session confirmed with a document uploaded. No further action needed. The session is archived and exportable.
On Hold Session paused — daily overdue notifications are suppressed. Use when waiting for paperwork. Resume restarts the normal workflow and notification schedule.
Cancelled Session permanently terminated. Scanned products are retained in the audit trail but the session is inactive and no confirmation is expected.
Overdue Notifications

Sessions that remain in Awaiting Confirmation for more than 7 days trigger a daily overdue email to the warehouse team. The counter stops when the session is closed, put on hold, or cancelled. When you put a session on hold and later resume it, the 7-day counter resets so notifications restart on the correct schedule.

Uploading Confirmation Attachments
Attaching a document to officially close a session

A session moves from Awaiting Confirmation to Closed when you upload a confirmation document. This is typically a signed PDF or SAP export that serves as proof of stock verification.

1

Open an Awaiting session

Click any card in the Awaiting tab on the dashboard to open the session detail page.

2

Scroll to the Confirmation Document section

Find the upload panel showing a drag-and-drop zone labelled "Upload & Close Session".

3

Upload the file

Drag and drop the file onto the zone, or click to open a file picker. Accepted formats: PDF, Excel (.xlsx), Word (.docx), JPG, PNG. Maximum size: 50 MB. You can add optional notes about the document before uploading.

4

Session is automatically closed

After a successful upload the status changes to Closed. The salesperson receives a notification. The document is stored in the system and available for download any time from the session detail page.

⚠️ Only one attachment is stored per session. Uploading a new file replaces the existing one. Always verify you are uploading the correct document before confirming.
✅ To download an existing attachment, open the session detail and click "Download" next to the file name. PDFs also display inline in the browser for quick review without downloading.
Remarks
Flagging products and sessions that need attention

Remarks let you communicate with the salesperson about specific products or the overall session — and vice versa.

TypeHow to addWhen to use
Product Remark Click any row in the products table. A slide-up panel opens with the full remark history and an Add Remark field. Flag a specific product — e.g. wrong expiry, missing serial, damaged packaging, quantity mismatch.
Session Remark Click "Add Remark" in the Batch Remarks section at the bottom of the session detail page. Note something about the whole session — e.g. partial scan, data discrepancy, request for re-scan.
ℹ️ Product rows with remarks are highlighted in amber in the products table so they are easy to spot during review. Remarks are visible to both salesperson and warehouse. They are part of the audit trail and cannot be deleted.
Session Actions
Force Complete, Reject, On Hold, Resume, and Terminate
ActionWhen to useWhat happens
Force Complete Session is Active but the salesperson has stopped scanning and is not responding. Status changes to Awaiting Confirmation. Logged as a forced completion by warehouse. Salesperson is notified.
Reject Session is Awaiting but the scanned data has errors the salesperson must fix. Status returns to Active. Any uploaded attachment is removed. Salesperson is notified to correct and resubmit.
Put on Hold Session is Awaiting and you are waiting for additional paperwork — you don't want daily overdue emails during the wait. Status changes to On Hold. Daily overdue notifications are suppressed. Session remains in the Awaiting tab with an On Hold badge.
Resume Session is On Hold and you are ready to proceed with confirmation. Status returns to Awaiting Confirmation. The overdue notification schedule restarts from today.
Terminate Session must be permanently cancelled — wrong hospital, duplicate session, or the visit is voided. Status changes to Cancelled. Both parties receive a termination notification. This action cannot be undone.
🚫 Terminate is permanent. For temporary pauses always use Put on Hold instead. Only terminate when the session must be permanently voided.

H2H Transfer — Initiating a Transfer
Scanning products moving from one hospital to another

When stock physically moves from one hospital to another, use the Hospital-to-Hospital (H2H) Transfer scanner. Start it from the session type toggle on the scanner form, or go directly to /product_scanning/transfers/new/.

1

Select From Hospital and To Hospital

Choose the source hospital (where stock currently is) and the destination hospital (where it is going). The two hospitals must be different.

2

Click "Start Transfer"

A transfer session is created with a unique Transfer ID. The transfer route (From → To) is displayed at the top of the scanner area for confirmation.

3

Scan each product being transferred

Use camera or scanner gun (same as normal scanning) to scan every product physically moving. Products appear in the list with serial, GTIN, batch, and expiry details.

4

Click "Complete Transfer"

The transfer session is submitted with status Awaiting Confirmation. The warehouse team will review the transfer and upload a confirmation document.

ℹ️ Transfer sessions follow the same status lifecycle as normal sessions: Active → Awaiting Confirmation → Closed. The warehouse team can also Force Complete, Reject, Put on Hold, and Terminate transfer sessions.

GTIN Material Master
Mapping product barcodes to SAP material codes

The GTIN Master at /product_scanning/gtin-material-master/ maps each product barcode (GTIN/EAN) to its SAP material code and description. When the scanner reads a barcode and finds the GTIN in this master, the Material Code and Product Name are auto-filled — the salesperson does not need to type them.

Tips & Troubleshooting
Common issues and how to resolve them
{% if role == 'salesperson' or role == 'admin' %} {% endif %} {% if role == 'warehouse' or role == 'admin' %} {% endif %}
IssueSolution
Camera won't scan the barcode Ensure good lighting; hold 10–15 cm from the code; clean the camera lens. Use the Manual Entry panel to type the barcode value directly if the camera cannot read it.
Hospital is missing from the dropdown Your email address is not assigned to that hospital in the Hospital Master. Contact the warehouse team with your exact login email so they can update the mapping.
Material Code is blank after a scan The GTIN is not yet in the GTIN Master. Note the barcode and report it to the warehouse team. You can also type the Material Code manually using the Manual Entry panel.
"Duplicate scan" error for a new product The same serial number was already scanned in this session. If the original scan was incorrect, delete it from the scanned list first, then re-scan the correct product.
Lost scan data after a browser refresh Products already saved to the server are safe. Reopen the scanner — the app will offer to resume your active session with all previously saved products.
Session was rejected — what do I do? Resume the rejected session from the scanner, fix the issues flagged in the remarks, and resubmit using "Complete & Submit".
Session stuck in Awaiting for days If the document is ready, upload it to close the session. If you're still waiting for paperwork, use Put on Hold to stop overdue emails, then Resume when the document arrives.
Product shows "Unknown Material" Add the GTIN to the GTIN Master, then edit the product record inside the session detail to assign the material code manually for this instance.
Salesperson cannot see their hospital Go to Hospital Master → find the hospital → check the Salesperson Email field exactly matches the salesperson's login email (verify domain, no extra spaces, no typos).
Attachment upload fails Check the file is under 50 MB and is one of the accepted formats (PDF, Excel, Word, JPG, PNG). Compress the PDF or reduce image resolution if needed.
Bulk import shows unexpected "skipped" rows Skipped rows mean the record already exists with identical data (no update needed), or a required field was blank. Check the error detail in the results summary below the import panel.
403 Forbidden / Access Denied page Your account does not have the required role group assigned. Contact the system administrator to check your Django group membership.
Page not loading or displaying incorrectly Try a hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows / Cmd+Shift+R on Mac. If the issue persists, report it via the Feedback Portal with your browser details.
Notification bell not updating Ensure your browser allows JavaScript on this site. Try logging out and back in — the notification count is refreshed on login.
💬 For bugs, data issues, or feature requests use the Feedback & Support portal. Include your role, browser, device, and steps to reproduce — this helps the team resolve your ticket faster.
