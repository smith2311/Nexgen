MeriTrack User Guide

Everything you need to know about scanning, inventory, and session management

🔬 MT Rep (Salesperson) 📊 MT Manager / Logistics
🚀 Getting Started — Starting a Scan Session ▼
  1. 1
    Open the Scanner

    Go to MeriTrack → Scanner from the navigation. You'll see a 3-step setup wizard.

  2. 2
    Select Hospital & Department

    Choose the hospital you're visiting from the dropdown. Then pick the department (ward/ICU/OT). Only your assigned hospitals appear if you're a pure rep.

  3. 3
    Choose Session Type

    Audit — count and verify existing consignment stock. Replenishment — scan items you're adding to the shelf. Removal — scan expired or recalled items being taken off shelf. Transfer — move stock to another hospital (H2H).

  4. 4
    Start the Session

    Click Start Audit. A session ID is auto-generated (e.g. MT-XXXXXXXXX). You're now in scanning mode.

📷 Scanning Products ▼
  1. 1
    Choose Input Mode

    Toggle between 📷 Camera (phone camera) and 🔫 Scanner Gun (hardware barcode reader). Camera mode works best for QR / DataMatrix codes. Scanner gun works best for linear barcodes.

  2. 2
    Scan the Barcode

    Point the camera at the product barcode. The app reads GS1 data automatically — extracting serial number, GTIN, batch, and expiry date from a single scan.

  3. 3
    Two-Scan Workflow

    Some products have a separate serial number barcode. If only a serial is detected, a banner appears saying "Serial Captured". Scan the product label next to complete the entry automatically.

  4. 4
    No-Serial Products

    Toggle Skip Serial if the product has no individual serial number (e.g. consumables). The app assigns an internal placeholder ID.

  5. 5
    Choose Action

    For each scan, select: ✓ Verify (audit check), + Add to Shelf (replenishment), ✕ Remove Expired/Recalled, or ♥ Used on Patient.

💡 Tip — Pinch to Zoom Use two fingers to pinch-zoom the camera view on mobile for small barcodes.
⚠️ Duplicate Prevention The same serial number cannot be scanned twice in the same session. The app will alert you if a duplicate is detected.
✅ Completing & Submitting a Session ▼
  1. 1
    Review Scanned Products

    After scanning, click Finish to go to the Review step. Check the product list — you can remove any incorrect entries.

  2. 2
    Add Session Notes (optional)

    Type any notes or observations about the visit. These appear in the session record for the manager to see.

  3. 3
    Submit the Session

    Click Complete & Submit. The session moves to Awaiting Confirmation status and the manager is notified.

💡 What happens next? A manager will review the session and either close it (confirmed) or reject it back to you for corrections.
🔄 H2H Transfers (Hospital-to-Hospital) ▼
  1. 1
    Start a Transfer

    Open the Scanner and set Session Type to 🔄 H2H Transfer. Select the source hospital (Hospital) and the destination hospital (Destination Hospital).

  2. 2
    Scan Items Being Transferred

    Scan each product that is moving from one hospital to the other. The transfer ID is auto-generated (e.g. DDMMYY-REP-FROM-TO).

  3. 3
    Complete the Transfer

    Click Complete Transfer when all items are scanned. The manager can then upload a confirmation document to close the transfer.

🔍 Filtering Sessions ▼
  1. 1
    Type Filter Pills

    Click All Types / Audit / Replenishment / Removal / Transfer to narrow the session cards by session type.

  2. 2
    Advanced Filters (⚙ Filters button)

    Click the ⚙ Filters button to open the filter panel. Filter by: Year, Month, Specific Date, Rep email, or Hospital name. A badge shows how many filters are active.

  3. 3
    Clear Filters

    Click ✕ Clear All in the filter panel to reset all advanced filters at once.

⚙️ Session Lifecycle & Manager Actions ▼
StatusMeaningActions available
Active Rep is scanning ✅ Force Complete, 🚫 Terminate
Awaiting Submitted, pending manager review 🔒 Close, ↩ Reject, ⏸ Hold
On Hold Paused — awaiting hospital paperwork ▶ Resume
Confirmed Closed and confirmed View only
Cancelled Terminated View only
↩ Reject vs 🚫 Terminate Reject sends the session back to Active so the rep can add/remove items. Terminate cancels it permanently.
⏸ Hold / ▶ Resume Use Hold when you're waiting on hospital paperwork and don't want follow-up notifications. Resume moves it back to Awaiting.
⚠️ Expiry Alert Management ▼

The Expiry Alert tab shows all consigned products on shelf that are expiring or already expired.

  1. 1
    Filter by Urgency

    Use the tier pills: Expired / ≤1 Week / ≤1 Month / ≤3 Months / ≤6 Months / Upcoming to focus on the most critical items.

  2. 2
    Acknowledge Items

    Click Ack next to an item to mark it as acknowledged. This creates a paper trail confirming you're aware of the expiry.

  3. 3
    Export CSV

    Click ⬇ Export CSV to download the current expiry list for reporting or SAP reconciliation.

🏥 Hospital & Product Catalog Management ▼
  1. 1
    Import / Export Hospitals

    Go to Hospitals → Hospital Master. Import a CSV/Excel to bulk-create or update hospitals. Export the template to get the correct column format.

  2. 2
    Assign Rep to Hospital

    Set the Salesperson Email field on each hospital to assign a rep. That rep will see only their assigned hospitals in the scanner.

  3. 3
    Product Catalog

    Go to Product Catalog to manage the master list of Meril products. Import from Excel or add/edit individually. GTIN codes in the catalog are used to auto-fill product names during scanning.
