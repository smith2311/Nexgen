{% comment %} The document detail modal — what opens when you click any amount in the grid. Read-only on purpose, and deliberately not the edit drawer. The drawer exists to change a record; this exists to answer "why does this row say that". Nobody should have to open an editable form, with 204 live inputs in it, to find out why a remark was generated. It floats above the records screen and never navigates away, so the row you were looking at is still there when you close it. The verdict band at the top is generated by the classifier itself (the rule id and its sentence), not by a second description of the rules living in the template — those two would drift the first time a rule changed. {% endcomment %}
