“NA” means not mandatory, not forbidden. A source marked NA that turns out to be present is never flagged — checked against the real 10,424-row export, at least eight billing types carry an NA source on almost every row (every ZCFU consignment challan has an E-Invoice despite EINV = NA). Only a missing mandatory source counts against a document. An NA source is also left out of the amount comparison, which is what stops 711 perfectly normal branch-transfer challans — GST deliberately Rs 0, E-Way Bill carrying the real goods value — from reading as mismatches.
