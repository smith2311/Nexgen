Deliberately standalone rather than {% templatetag openblock %} extends "base.html" {% templatetag closeblock %}: a vendor is not a Meril employee, and base.html's chrome is the internal platform's — an app switcher, a notification bell, a sidebar of modules they have no access to. They get their own topbar and nothing else. Colours are the VendorLink design system's, lifted as named custom properties rather than linking vendorlink-ds.css: that stylesheet scopes every token to the `.vlx` class and carries ~2,500 lines for screens this page does not have. The values below are the same ones (--vlx-primary, --vlx-secondary, --vlx-gold and friends), so the portal and the rest of VendorLink stay in step visually without this page inheriting a component library it uses four classes of. Gold, not green, marks a win. That is the design system's own rule — green is "approved/active", amber is "needs attention", and a winning quote is neither. {% endcomment %} Vendor Portal — Meril VendorLink
