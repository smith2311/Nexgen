VendorLink has no audit-log table, so the activity *is* the business rows: this screen reads the twelve tables that carry a user FK (see vendorlink/useractivity.py) and stitches them into one timeline. Deliberately four panels, not nine. An earlier cut also carried a work-mix donut, an actions-by-role donut, a weekday column, vendors-by-country and bids-by-currency. Every one of those either restated something already on the page (the trend is stacked by work type, so the donut was the same breakdown twice; the heatmap already carries the weekday axis) or was vendor-master trivia rather than an answer to "what is each user doing". They are gone. Charts are Highcharts, vendored — nothing is fetched at runtime.
