{% comment %} The "robot is writing a report" loader, shown while an OpenAI call is running. Used in three shapes: * `--overlay` — covers the page for the ?ai=1 report links, which are ordinary navigations that block on the model. Those had no feedback at all: you clicked and stared at the old page for ten seconds. * `--inline` — inside the AI Bid Evaluator panel while it polls. * `--bubble` — inside a MerilGPT chat bubble while the answer is in flight. Inline SVG, animated with CSS keyframes, and no library: the whole scene is a dozen shapes, and pulling in an animation dependency to move them would cost more than it draws. Every animation is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion — see .vlx-ailoader in vendorlink-ds.css. Caller may pass `message`. There is deliberately no percentage or progress bar: the OpenAI call reports no progress, so a bar would be inventing one. The elapsed counter that vl-ailoader.js fills in is real. {% endcomment %}
